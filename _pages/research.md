Social Contingency
======
Social contingency broadly refers to the temporal promptness and contextual relevance of responses in social interactions. From early infancy, children are sensitive to contingency in their interactions mainly with caregivers, which shapes their attribution of agency, modulates their social engagement, and contributes to their development across multiple domains.

Characterization
------
The precise criteria for promptness and relevance remain insufficiently understood and may vary substantially across modalities and cultures. Using an experimental paradigm that enables infant–caregiver dyads to engage in screen-mediated mutual gaze within a 2-D virtual space, we investigate the spatiotemporal structure of gaze interactions to identify the features that characterize contingent social interactions.

For details, see our [first paper](https://doi.org/10.1098/rstb.2024.0376).
